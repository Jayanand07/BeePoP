Our story

Pure goodness, in every drop.

"Nature's sweetest gift."

Real blossoms. Our honey comes from small apiaries across Bengal, where the bees forage on mustard, litchi, and wild forest flowers — never fed sugar syrup, never rushed. Each harvest is single-origin, so the taste of the season is preserved in every stick.

Real bees, real care. We cold-extract and minimally strain — no high-heat pasteurization, no ultra-filtration, no additives. What's in the stick is what was in the comb: nothing added, nothing taken away, nothing to hide.
